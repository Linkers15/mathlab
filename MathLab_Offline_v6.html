Algebra reference

Distributive law
a(b + c) = ab + ac

Quadratic formula
x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a

Difference of squares
a² − b² = (a − b)(a + b)

Scope of the CAS

Polynomial tools accept arbitrary nonnegative integer powers, subject to a practical complexity limit. Real and complex higher-degree roots and factors may be approximate. Vertex form exists specifically for quadratics. Cancellation preserves equality only where the original denominator is nonzero.

Precision & limits

Symbolic differentiation uses algebraic rules. Antiderivatives use a finite rule set. Definite integrals use numerical quadrature; limits are numerical estimates, not rigorous proofs.
